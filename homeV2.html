Industry & Technology Transfer

In 2019, I joined a technology startup in 3D vision sensors as Chief Scientist. I led work on suppressing complex ambient illumination and reconstructing highly reflective objects, advancing high-precision 3D scanning from core algorithms to engineered products and market deployment. My industrial R&D also covered 3D-vision-guided robotic systems.

Recognition associated with these product and entrepreneurship efforts includes:

  • 湖南省标志性创新产品奖
  • 广东省“众创杯”创新创业大赛科技海归领航赛银奖
  • 第十二届中国深圳创新创业大赛先进制造行业决赛企业组优秀奖
