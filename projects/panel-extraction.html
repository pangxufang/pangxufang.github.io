A Robust Panel Extraction Method for Manga

Xufang Pang, Ying Cao, Rynson W.H. Lau, and Antoni B. Chan

ACM Multimedia · pp. 1125–1128 · 2014

Paper (PDF) Publication Code (RAR)

Overview

This method separates manga pages into individual panels despite irregular layouts, joined regions, and open borders. It repairs missing boundaries, builds a background mask, recursively splits the remaining panel regions, and recovers their shapes. The extracted panels can support reading on small displays.
