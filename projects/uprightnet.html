Learning Upright Orientation for 3D Point Cloud

Upright-Net and Upright-Net+ estimate upright orientation in 3D point clouds by learning the points that constitute the natural base of a 3D model.

We reformulate continuous orientation estimation as a discrete classification task by learning the points that form an object’s natural base. This functional structure stabilizes the object in its typical pose according to physical laws and geometric properties.
