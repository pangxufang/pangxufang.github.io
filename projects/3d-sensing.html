Research & Technology Translation · Xufang Pang (庞旭芳)

High-Precision 3D Sensing for Robotic Guidance

My work in 3D sensing connects scanning-system calibration, geometric processing, and 3D vision guidance for robotic operations. This research draws on experience with 3D sensors and industrial machine vision systems.

Research Foundation

  • Scanning-system calibration: a tool-free calibration method for turntable-based 3D scanning systems.
  • Geometric feature extraction: extracting and enhancing valley-ridge features from point sets, including point-cloud-based 3D fingerprint models.
  • Point cloud understanding: Upright-Net and Upright-Net+ learn functional structures to estimate upright orientation.

Industrial R&D

In 2019, I joined a technology startup in 3D vision sensors as Chief Scientist. My work addressed complex ambient illumination and highly reflective objects, advancing 3D scanning algorithms toward engineered products. The industrial R&D also covered 3D-vision-guided robotic systems.

Current Research

I am investigating high-precision 3D perception for fine robotic operations, dynamic reconstruction in unstructured environments, and structured spatial representations that combine geometry, semantics, motion, interactions, and physical attributes. Video-driven interactive 3D scene generation and embodied world modeling are ongoing research directions.

Representative Papers

  • A Tool-Free Calibration Method for Turntable-Based 3D Scanning Systems — calibration for turntable-based scanning.
  • Extracting Valley-Ridge Lines from Point-Cloud-Based 3D Fingerprint Models — geometric line features from 3D fingerprint point clouds.
  • Upright-Net / Upright-Net+ — learning upright orientation from point cloud structure.
