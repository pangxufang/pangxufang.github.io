DynamicManga: Animating Still Manga via Camera Movement

Ying Cao, Xufang Pang, Antoni B. Chan, and Rynson W.H. Lau

IEEE Transactions on Multimedia · 19(1):160–172 · 2017

Paper (PDF) Publication Supplementary material Original project

Overview

DynamicManga turns existing manga pages into animations using virtual camera movement. It extracts panels, characters, and balloons, estimates motion and emotion for each panel, and plans camera movements around the story. The approach supports mobile comic reading and comic trailer creation.
